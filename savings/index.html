The formula, with your numbers

Every figure above comes from these three lines — nothing else is added in.

(72W − 18W) × 8 hrs × 365 days ÷ 1,000 = 157.7 kWh / year
157.7 kWh × $0.12 per kWh = $18.92 / year
$18.92 × 1 fixture = $18.92 total per year

General form:  (Existing watts − LED watts) × hours/day × days/year ÷ 1,000 = kWh saved, then kWh × rate = dollars saved. One kilowatt-hour (kWh) is 1,000 watts running for one hour — that is the unit your utility bill charges for.

A note on ballasts — these numbers are conservative

The calculation uses the lamp wattages printed on the fixtures. In practice, fluorescent, high-pressure sodium, and metal halide fixtures also draw power through a ballast, so the existing fixture usually pulls more than its nameplate watts — a 400W metal halide system typically draws about 458W, and a 1,000W system about 1,080W. Your real savings will generally be higher than the figures shown here.

Maintenance savings are not included either: LED fixtures typically last 50,000 hours or more, so relamping and ballast-replacement costs on the old fixtures disappear as well.
